2 · The problem

AAC devices are where language lives — and where data goes to die.

  • Children with cerebral palsy, autism, or apraxia communicate by pressing symbols — every press is a word.
  • Those presses are rich longitudinal data — and today they evaporate. Teachers guess. Therapists see 40 minutes a week.
  • Worse: the obvious analytics are clinically dangerous. "Move her favourite button closer!" destroys the motor plan she spent months building — like rearranging a blind person's furniture.

These children talk by pressing symbols. Every press is data — and today it evaporates. But here's the trap: naive analytics on this data produces actively harmful advice. Moving a child's most-used button "closer" destroys months of muscle memory. So the product question isn't "what can we show" — it's "what must we refuse to say".

4 · The unusual part

Eight clinical constraints, enforced in schema and code — not in copy.

Never move or resize a learned buttonThe database rejects a "move" proposal at the schema level. The UI offers mask and add-a-copy only.
Repeated pressing is communication, not errorrepeat_tap_rate is neutral by catalogue — it cannot render as good or bad. Stimming is not a bug.
A refusal is successful communicationThe "unused word" finding renders no action button — a child's "no" must count.
Forbidden actions are displayed, not just obeyedEvery finding card prints what practice rules out, so the adult learns the why.
Nothing is flagged before a baseline14 active days and 40 self-generated utterances — gated on activity, not the calendar.
Slow may be the adultPartner wait time is measured; a child is never called slow while the adult waits 1.4 s.

Here's what makes this project unusual. We read the AAC clinical literature and turned it into eight binding constraints that live in the schema and the query layer. The database rejects a "move this button" proposal — the AI cannot even propose it. Repetition is typed neutral, so no screen can paint stimming as a problem. A refusal finding has no action button, because a child's "no" has to count.

5 · The adult's side

A queue of who needs a human — not a wall of charts.

  • Attention queue ranks the class by open findings — Jonah first: silent for 5 days
  • Deterministic SQL rules, never a model — a therapist can trace every finding to a number
  • Every metric carries its sample size — a number without n doesn't render
  • Unmeasured things say "not recorded" — never a fabricated zero

The teacher's side opens with a queue, not charts: who needs a human today, and why. Jonah's on top — he hasn't spoken in five days. The findings come from plain SQL rules, deliberately not a model, so a therapist can trace every claim to a number. Every figure carries its sample size, and what isn't measured says "not recorded" — never a fake zero.

6 · Findings that argue their case

Evidence, thresholds, and a "no" button that trains the rules.

  • Each card shows the numbers that fired it and the thresholds they crossed
  • "Does that match what you see?" — dismissals with reasons feed the rule engine
  • What practice forbids is printed on the card in red
  • One-click PDF — a real file for the IEP meeting, caveats included

Each finding argues its case: the exact numbers, the thresholds they crossed, and — in red — what practice forbids doing about it. The human always decides: "does that match what you see?", and a "no" with a reason feeds back into the rules. The whole report exports as a real PDF, caveats included, for the IEP meeting.

7 · The AI layer

An agentic analyst that cannot leak and cannot harm.

  • Multi-step tool loop — this answer made 11 calls, 5 children in parallel
  • Scope injected server-side — the model never chooses whose data it reads
  • A guard rewrites any answer recommending what practice forbids
  • Gemini 3 · GPT-5.1 · Claude Sonnet 5 — swap model + keys in Settings; the guardrails don't move

The Ask panel is a real agent: this table took eleven tool calls — five children queried in parallel. But scope is injected server-side; the model never picks whose data it reads. A guard checks every answer against the forbidden list and rewrites violations. And it's multi-provider — Gemini 3 by default, GPT or Claude with a key in Settings. Swap the brain; the guardrails stay.
